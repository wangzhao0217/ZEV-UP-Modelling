# Source and data rights

This packaging change does not assign a new software licence or transfer
copyright ownership. The repository maintainer must choose the appropriate
software licence after confirming contributor/project rights. Public visibility
alone should not be described as an open-source licence grant.

The small NTS aggregate assumption record identifies Department for Transport
public-sector information under the Open Government Licence v3.0; retain its
attribution and provenance. Other providers' source or derived data retain
applicable terms and are not relicensed by this repository.

The two illustrative CSVs were constructed for rule testing, contain no real
records or locations, and are clearly distinguished from the national inputs.
